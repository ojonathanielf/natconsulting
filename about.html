The Founder

Nathaniel Ojo & Nat Consulting

Nat Consulting Ltd. was born out of a deep passion for education, Biblical study, and human development. Founded by Nathaniel Ojo — educator, author, and emotional intelligence consultant — the organisation exists to create resources that equip people to live with greater purpose, wisdom, and self-awareness.

With a background spanning Christian education, curriculum development, and EQ coaching, Nathaniel has spent years bridging the gap between spiritual formation and practical self-leadership. He believes that when Scripture-based wisdom meets emotional intelligence, the result is a life of extraordinary impact.

Today, Nat Consulting serves students, readers, professionals, and organisations across Nigeria and beyond — through published workbooks, consulting engagements, and training workshops that make lasting change accessible to all.
